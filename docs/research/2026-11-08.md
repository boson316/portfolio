## Model configuration

Qwen3-ASR-0.6B with CUDA graph decoding; MERaLiON-SER-v1 on GPU; YuNet plus MediaPipe and ORSANet large; confidence-weighted facial, voice, and semantic fusion; Qwen3.6-35B-A3B with AWQ 4-bit and vLLM shared by semantics and dialogue; streaming Ming-omni-tts-0.5B with Qwen3-TTS-1.7B fallback. This lists the system's components without asserting that every model executes on the Jetson hardware.

The facial weights mix the original RAF-DB epoch-54 weights with fine-tuned weights equally. Deployed since October 26; November 8 adds class biases, not new base weights.

## Keep separate or omit

- Earlier fear recall of 82.6% precedes November 8 biases and came with only 33.8% precision. The current pair is 35.4% precision and 80.2% recall.
- Older KDEF 80.7% covers 2,934 frontal and 45-degree images, excludes 90-degree views, and predates November 8 biases. It is not directly comparable to latest all-angle accuracy. Not needed in the compact card.
- Do not publish metrics for newly added or license-unconfirmed datasets. The compact card uses FER+ rather than those results.
- Omit literature comparisons, claims of best published results, elderly clinical outcomes, and unverified Taiwanese-accent improvements.
- Fusion temperatures remain 1, with no calibration or overall multimodal accuracy evaluation. No older-adult facial test set is available.
- Browser microphone input, caregiver notifications, and the full HDMI demonstration are not implemented. The system currently receives dialogue audio through the Orin microphone.
